No maths brain required

Trigonometry,
made simple

Three funny words — sin, cos, tan — are just three ways of dividing one side of a triangle by another. That is the whole secret.

Step 1 · the only shape that matters

The right triangle

A right triangle has one perfect square corner (90°). Pick one of the other corners and call it θ — "theta" just means the angle I care about. Then the three sides get names.

Hypotenuse — the slanted side. Always the longest. Opposite — the side across the room from θ. It never touches θ. Adjacent — the side leaning against θ. "Adjacent" means next door.
Step 2 · the magic words

SOH · CAH · TOA

Say it out loud like a spell: "soh-kah-toa". Each word is a fraction. Divide one side by another and the answer is sin, cos or tan.

SOH
sin θ = Opposite ÷ Hypotenuse

Sine = how far it goes UP.

CAH
cos θ = Adjacent ÷ Hypotenuse

Cosine = how far it goes ACROSS.

TOA
tan θ = Opposite ÷ Adjacent

Tangent = how STEEP it is.

Note: "cosine" and "cos" are the same thing. Cos is just the short way to write it, like "phone" and "telephone".

Step 3 · play with it

Drag the angle

The slanted side stays 1 unit long. Open the angle and the triangle stands up taller and gets narrower — and the three numbers change with it. That is all sin, cos and tan ever do.

sin θ0.50
cos θ0.87
tan θ0.58
Step 4 · where waves come from

The spinning wheel

Put the triangle inside a circle and spin it. The height of the moving dot draws a wave — that wave is sine. Its side-to-side shadow draws the same wave starting earlier — that is cosine. Sound, radio, tides, springs and heartbeats all wiggle exactly like this.

Step 5 · the three lazy cousins

cosec, sec and cot

These scare people for no reason. They are the first three turned upside down. Flip the fraction, done.

cosec θ
1 ÷ sin θ = Hyp ÷ Opp

Sine flipped. Also written csc.

sec θ
1 ÷ cos θ = Hyp ÷ Adj

Cosine flipped.

cot θ
1 ÷ tan θ = Adj ÷ Opp

Tangent flipped. Roofers use it: how far across for every 1 up?

Step 6 · why anyone bothers

Real life, real numbers

Trigonometry exists for one reason: measuring things you cannot reach. Measure the easy part, and the triangle tells you the rest.

🪜

The ladder — use sine

Your ladder is 6 m long and leans at 65°. Will it reach the 5 m window?

height = 6 × sin 65° = 6 × 0.906 = 5.44 m ✅ it reaches
🌳

The tree — use tangent

You stand 20 m from a tree and tilt your head up 40° to see the top. How tall is it?

height = 20 × tan 40° = 20 × 0.839 = 16.8 m
♿

The ramp — use sine and cot

A wheelchair ramp must rise 0.5 m at a gentle 5°. How long is the ramp, and how much floor does it use?

ramp length = 0.5 ÷ sin 5° = 5.74 m floor used = 0.5 × cot 5° = 5.72 m
🎡

Ferris wheel & headphones — the wave

A wheel of radius 15 m turns once a minute. Your height above the middle is 15 × sin(angle so far). The same equation describes a speaker cone, a guitar string, the electricity in your wall and the tide at the beach.

after 10 seconds: 15 × sin 60° = 13 m up
📱

Your phone, all day long

GPS finds you by solving triangles to satellites. Games use sin and cos to move a character in the direction it faces. Maps rotate with sin and cos. Every 3D render does this thousands of times a second.

walking speed 5, heading 30°: across = 5 × cos 30° = 4.33 up = 5 × sin 30° = 2.50
